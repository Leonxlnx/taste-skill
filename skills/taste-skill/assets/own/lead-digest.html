The footbridge reopens

12 Nov · Wick Valley Field Notes

The crossing at Mill Beck is open again. The new deck follows the old footbridge between the orchard bank and the meadow. Walkers can use the crossing from either bank.

Orchard gate moved

9 Nov · Wick Valley Field Notes

The entrance on the west side of the orchard has moved. From the lane, follow the hedge to the new opening. Close the gate behind you.

Two winter paths

4 Nov · Wick Valley Field Notes

Both paths across the upper meadow remain open for winter. One follows the hedge and the other cuts across to the ridge. Use either route on the way back from the beck.

Ferry hours change

28 Oct · Wick Valley Field Notes

The valley ferry is on its winter timetable. The final boat leaves at 16:30. Arrive at the landing before that departure to cross the beck.
