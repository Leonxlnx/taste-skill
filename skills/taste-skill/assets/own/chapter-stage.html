Binding a 192-page book

Bracken Bookbindery sews the pages, lines the spine with cloth and fits a board case.

Sew the signatures Line the spine Fit the case
  1. Step 1 of 3

    Sew the signatures

    Gather the 192 pages into 12 signatures of 16. Sew through each fold to join the book block.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    Line the spine

    Brush glue over the sewn folds and press a strip of cloth across the spine.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    Fit the case

    Set the lined block into its case and secure it between two 2 mm boards.
