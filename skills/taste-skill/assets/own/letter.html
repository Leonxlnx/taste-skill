Repairs cost more from March. Here is why.

12 January 2027

Dear customers,

most of you send us a book that has been read to pieces: a cookbook with a broken spine, a hymnal with loose pages, a first edition someone dropped in the bath. We have mended about 900 of them since we opened the workshop in 2009.

For fifteen years a spine repair cost 45 €. The leather and the linen thread we use now cost twice what they did then, and a good repair still takes a full afternoon. From 1 March a spine repair costs 62 €; a new cover stays at 90 €.

What we cannot do faster: a repair still takes three to four weeks, because the glue needs its days in the press. We would rather tell you that now than in March.

Every book already on our bench, or booked before the end of February, is mended at the old price. Thank you for trusting us with the ones you cannot replace.

IH
