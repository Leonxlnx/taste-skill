Ordering and caring for your mugs

Answers from the Reeve Ceramics studio.

OrderingDeliveryCare

Ordering

How many mugs can I order?

The minimum order is one mug.

Can I order a custom colour?

Choose a custom glaze colour when you order 12 mugs or more.

Delivery

When will my mugs ship?

We dispatch orders within three working days.

When can I collect my order?

Collect from the studio on Friday between 14:00 and 17:00.

Care

Can the mugs go in the dishwasher?

Yes. Place them on the top rack.

Can I keep using a chipped mug?

Retire a chipped mug from drink service.
