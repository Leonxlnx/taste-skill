The Pellin field recorder in four parts

A pocket recorder for interviews and birdsong, in the order you meet it: what you hear, what you hold, how long it runs, what you take home.

1 Sound

Two capsules, crossed. A room sounds like the room.

The pair sits at 90 degrees, so a speaker stays in the middle and the street stays at the side.

−18 dB average, −6 dB peaks

2 Body

One hand, no menu. Record is the largest key.

Four keys and a dial for the level, all reachable with the thumb that holds it.

118 × 46 × 21 mm, 140 g

3 Battery

A day out, then a night on the cable.

Two AA cells or the USB-C port; it records while it charges.

16 hours on two AA cells

4 Files

Files named by place and time.

Each take is saved as a WAV file the moment you stop, named so you can find it a year later.

  • kitchen-0612-1930.wav48:02
