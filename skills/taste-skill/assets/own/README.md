# Content-shaped sections

Each of the first thirty-five worked examples contains one section with scoped styles and a module where needed. Open them together in [the first gallery](index.html) (the first twelve and the three story examples), [gallery A](gallery-own-a.html) and [gallery B](gallery-own-b.html). Move the styles into the page’s `#own-css` block after the kit stylesheets and place the module after the section. Keep the relationship between the content and the layout, and replace the sample copy and figures with the client’s own. Find the example by what the content is, below.

Rebuild the three galleries and the section library links from the repository root with `node skills/taste-skill/scripts/section-index.mjs`.

## Steps, states and chapters

| Example | Content it suits | What to change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Rail](rail.html) | A process with a deliverable at every stop. | Rewrite the steps and handoffs. Keep durations only when supplied in the brief. |
| [States](states.html) | One object held through three to five states (a return, an order, an incident). | Keep one record’s id and fields in every card; change the steps, times and the field that moves. |
| [Chapter stage](chapter-stage.html) | Stages of a physical change. | Replace the three accounts and drawings, keeping the subject at the same scale and angle. |
| [Chapter links](chapter-links.html) | A guide with a retained reference sheet. | Change chapter anchors, sketches and instructions together with the printable chart. |
| [Margin track](margin-track.html) | A run of chapters on one product, use first and machine last. | Change the stops, chapter heads, drawings and facts together; keep one stop per chapter. |
| [Day bar](day-bar.html) | Recurring hours on the same clock for a week. | Change the open and close labels and their hour columns, starting at grid line 1 for 08:00. |
