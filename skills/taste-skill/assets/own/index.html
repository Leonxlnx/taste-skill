Repairs cost more from March. Here is why.

12 January 2027

Dear customers,

most of you send us a book that has been read to pieces: a cookbook with a broken spine, a hymnal with loose pages, a first edition someone dropped in the bath. We have mended about 900 of them since we opened the workshop in 2009.

For fifteen years a spine repair cost 45 €. The leather and the linen thread we use now cost twice what they did then, and a good repair still takes a full afternoon. From 1 March a spine repair costs 62 €; a new cover stays at 90 €.

What we cannot do faster: a repair still takes three to four weeks, because the glue needs its days in the press. We would rather tell you that now than in March.

Every book already on our bench, or booked before the end of February, is mended at the old price. Thank you for trusting us with the ones you cannot replace.

IH

Ines HollinBookbinder, Hollin Bindery

The Pellin field recorder in four parts

A pocket recorder for interviews and birdsong, in the order you meet it: what you hear, what you hold, how long it runs, what you take home.

1 Sound

Two capsules, crossed. A room sounds like the room.

The pair sits at 90 degrees, so a speaker stays in the middle and the street stays at the side.

−18 dB average, −6 dB peaks

2 Body

One hand, no menu. Record is the largest key.

Four keys and a dial for the level, all reachable with the thumb that holds it.

118 × 46 × 21 mm, 140 g

3 Battery

A day out, then a night on the cable.

Two AA cells or the USB-C port; it records while it charges.

16 hours on two AA cells

4 Files

Files named by place and time.

Each take is saved as a WAV file the moment you stop, named so you can find it a year later.

  • marsh-0612-0514.wav12:40
  • kitchen-0612-1930.wav48:02

48 kHz, 24-bit WAV
