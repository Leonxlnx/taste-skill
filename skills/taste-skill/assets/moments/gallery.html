Moments

9 creative moments, one when the brief asks for motion or the temper is lively or loud, dealt by fit: each carries tones (warm, editorial, product, tech, playful) and an intensity, and every kind takes the tones and intensities that fit it (references/moments.md, The fit rule). Each link is a full demo page; the moment is marked data-moment in its source.

  • Closing lift transition · closing · scroll · lively · warm, editorial, product

    The last section uncovers the closing photograph. The photograph settles from a close crop, the line stays at full opacity and only the action fades.

    Phone: The closing photograph follows in ordinary flow. Its line stands at full opacity and its action fades in. Reduced motion: A plain closing section with the photograph, the line and the action.

  • Lines rise text · statement · enter · quiet · editorial, warm, product, tech, playful

    On a loud page a content heading rises line by line on first entry, with 90 ms between lines. The surrounding content stays readable. On an older section mount, marked parts follow the heading.

    Phone: The loud heading can rise once. Quiet and lively headings stay still. Reduced motion: The section stands as written.

  • Liquid hover liquid · hero, section · hover · bold · WebGL · tech, playful

    The pointer pushes a photograph like the surface of water: the picture bends along the pointer's path with a wet shine and a hair of colour split, and the ripple diffuses away in a second or two, back to the plain picture.

    Phone: The plain photograph, no canvas, no WebGL context. Reduced motion: The photograph stands; also on touch screens and without WebGL.

  • Marquee row scroll · section · loop · lively · playful, tech, warm, editorial, product

    One row of client words, trades, cities or formats moves slowly sideways in a loop, fading at both edges, the first word whole as it starts; it pauses under the pointer and has a pause button after the row, never over a word.

    Phone: It runs on a phone, slower and with a narrower fade, the pause button after the row. Reduced motion: The words stand in a wrapped row.

  • Photo chapters scroll · section · scroll · lively · warm, editorial, product

    Each step depends on the last and shows a distinct result. For independent details use a compact list or captioned views side by side.

    Phone: Each photograph flows above its title and description. Reduced motion: Nothing is pinned: the chapters stand side by side, each photograph over its title and lines, and the section is as tall as they are.

  • Picture develops transition · section, hero · enter · quiet · warm, editorial, product

    The photograph develops from grey to colour once, with the film look.

    Phone: The film look runs on a phone too (one picture, a filter and a transform, once); the print look shows the photograph. Reduced motion: The photograph stands.

  • Pinned chapters scroll · section · scroll · lively · product, editorial, tech, warm

    Each step depends on the last and shows a distinct result. For independent details use a compact list or captioned views side by side.

    Phone: Each complete step and its picture follow in order. Reduced motion: Each step stays with its text and complete visual. Desktop uses two columns and phones stack them.

  • Window parallax scroll · section · scroll · quiet · warm, editorial, product

    One wide photograph seen through a frame moves a little slower than the page, so the frame reads as a window onto a larger picture.

    Phone: It runs on a phone with two thirds of the travel: the photograph still moves a little slower than the page in its frame. Reduced motion: The photograph stands.

  • Statement lights up scroll · statement · scroll · quiet · warm, editorial, product, tech, playful

    A complete statement stands in readable muted ink. Words take full ink in reading order as the heading crosses the middle of the screen.

    Phone: The full heading stays in flow with the same change of ink. Reduced motion holds the final colour. Reduced motion: The statement in ink.
