Moments

17 creative moments, two a page on every kind (three on a long page), dealt by fit: each carries tones (warm, editorial, product, tech, playful) and an intensity, and every kind takes the tones and intensities that fit it (references/moments.md, The fit rule). Each link is a full demo page; the moment is marked data-moment in its source.

  • Closing lift transition · closing · scroll · lively · warm, editorial, product

    The last content section scrolls away like a sheet and uncovers the closing photograph held under it; the photograph settles from a close crop and the closing line with the one action fades up on it.

    Phone and reduced motion: A lighter version on a phone: nothing is held under the sheet; the closing photograph comes after it as a plain section, settling from its close crop as it rises into the screen, and the closing line fades up on it.

  • Counter rolls text · statement · enter · lively · tech, product, editorial, warm, playful

    The digits of two to four proof figures roll once like an odometer when the row comes on screen, the right-most digit turning most; the strips rest on the real figures before and after, so the row never reads as zeros.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone as on a desktop: the figures roll once as the row comes on screen.

  • Cursor spotlight hover · hero · pointer · quiet · tech, playful, product

    A soft light on the hero's ground follows the pointer with a short lag and shows a fine dot grid around it; it drifts back to its resting spot when the pointer leaves.

    Phone and reduced motion: The light rests at its spot; there is no pointer.

  • Giant type, picture passing scroll · statement · scroll · lively · editorial, warm, product, playful, tech

    Two to four words set at 12vw with a photograph showing through the letters under a 35% ink tint; the photograph passes upward through the type as the section crosses the screen.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone with the type smaller (19vw) and two thirds of the travel: the photograph still passes up through the letters.

  • Iris opens transition · section · scroll · lively · editorial, product, tech, playful, warm

    A section that sits on a new ground keeps that ground edge to edge; inside it an inset panel in the old ground (even gutter, card corners, never touching the section's edges) opens a disc in the middle of what is visible, widening with a hairline accent ring at its edge. Each heading, paragraph, button and picture stays hidden until the disc holds all of it, then fades in whole, so no word is ever cut. When the two grounds are too close to tell apart, the panel is moved lighter or darker until the disc reads, and the ring is 2px with a soft shade.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone as on a desktop: the inset panel in the old ground opens its disc as the section rises, each block fading in whole.

  • Letters assemble scroll · statement · scroll · lively · playful, tech, editorial, warm, product

    A statement of two to five words, set very large: its letters start scattered, shifted and turned, and fly into the line one after the other as the statement rises through the screen, whole before it reaches the middle.

    Phone and reduced motion: A lighter version on a phone, in a smaller size: no scrub; the letters start a third as far from home and fly into the line once, in 1.1 s, as the statement comes on screen.

  • Line carries the colour transition · section · scroll · lively · editorial, warm, product, tech

    A section that sits on a new ground keeps that ground edge to edge; inside it an inset panel in the old ground (even gutter, card corners, never touching the section's edges) is cleared by a one-pixel accent line that climbs through it from its foot to its top: below the line the section's own ground and words, above it the old ground. Each heading, paragraph, button and picture stays hidden until the line has passed above all of it, then fades in whole, so no word is ever cut. When the two grounds are too close to tell apart, the panel is moved lighter or darker until the change reads, and the line is 2px.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone as on a desktop: the accent line climbs through the inset panel as the section rises, each block fading in whole.

  • Lines rise text · statement · enter · quiet · editorial, warm, product, tech, playful

    One statement heading of two or three lines rises line by line out of masks the first time it comes on screen, 90 ms between lines; its lead, link and visual fade up behind it. Once. Slower and larger than the automatic section reveal, so it reads as the statement arriving.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone as on a desktop: the heading rises line by line once, the lines after it fade up.

  • Liquid hover liquid · hero, section · hover · bold · WebGL · tech, playful

    The pointer pushes a photograph like the surface of water: the picture bends along the pointer's path with a wet shine and a hair of colour split, and the ripple diffuses away in a second or two, back to the plain picture.

    Phone and reduced motion: The plain photograph, no canvas, no WebGL context.

  • Liquid type liquid · hero, statement · enter · lively · playful, tech

    A very large headline ripples and stretches like type seen through water: one ripple passes through it from left to right each time it comes on screen, the pointer ripples it wherever it crosses the letters, and a tap or a touch drag ripples it at the finger; it settles back to crisp type within about a second. One SVG filter, only while it moves.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone: the ripple passes through the headline as it comes on screen, and a tap or a touch drag ripples it again.

  • Marquee row scroll · section · loop · lively · playful, tech, warm, editorial, product

    One row of client words, trades, cities or formats moves slowly sideways in a loop, fading at both edges, the first word whole as it starts; it pauses under the pointer and has a pause button after the row, never over a word.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone, slower and with a narrower fade, the pause button after the row.

  • Photo chapters scroll · section · scroll · lively · warm, editorial, product

    A short story in three to five photographs, one large frame at a time: the section holds on screen while, under its h2, one photograph the width of the page is uncovered by the next from its foot upward, settling from a slight zoom, and a panel in the page's ground on the photograph's lower left changes to that chapter (its number, a title and two lines). Each chapter has about two thirds of a screen of scroll: the wipe takes under half of it and the photograph holds for the rest. Live at every width on a screen 600px or taller, phones included.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone held upright (one photograph at a time, the panel across its foot); a phone turned sideways, under 600px tall, gets the chapters stacked, each photograph over its words.

  • Picture develops transition · section, hero · enter · quiet · warm, editorial, product

    One large photograph develops once as it comes on screen. In its dealt look ("film") it comes up out of the paper like a print in the developer tray: a faint grey print at first, its darks arriving before its light tones, then its colour, while it settles from a slight zoom, about 1.6 s. The halftone look ("print", a two-tone dot print in the page's ground and ink giving way to the photograph) is not dealt; it is kept as opts look "print".

    Phone and reduced motion: The film look runs on a phone too (one picture, a filter and a transform, once); the print look shows the photograph.

  • Pinned chapters scroll · section · scroll · lively · product, editorial, tech, warm

    A feature section holds on screen for two screens of scroll while its three steps open one at a time at the left and one frame at the right changes to each step's picture with a short crossfade.

    Phone and reduced motion: A lighter version on a phone, nothing pinned: each step stands with its title, two lines and its visual under it; the step crossing the middle of the screen takes the accent rule at its left, and each picture settles from a close crop to its framing as it rises.

  • Window parallax scroll · section · scroll · quiet · warm, editorial, product

    One wide photograph seen through a frame moves a little slower than the page, so the frame reads as a window onto a larger picture.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone with two thirds of the travel: the photograph still moves a little slower than the page in its frame.

  • Statement lights up scroll · statement · scroll · quiet · warm, editorial, product, tech, playful

    One large statement rests at half ink (readable, about 4 to 1) and turns to ink word by word as it crosses the middle of the screen, a strong word to the accent; it runs back on the way up.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone as on a desktop (one paragraph's colours); under reduced motion the whole statement is in ink, the accent word in the accent.

  • Word rotates text · hero, statement, closing · loop · lively · playful, tech, product, warm

    One word of a headline keeps changing every two seconds while it is on screen, sliding up out of its mask as the next rises in, underlined in the accent, with a small pause button under the headline (it also pauses under the pointer). In a statement or the close ("Bread for breakfast / lunch / Sunday"), or the hero unless the hero is the word hero, which has its own live word. A comma or full stop after the word stays on its line.

    Phone and reduced motion: It runs on a phone as on a desktop: the word changes every two seconds, the pause button under the headline.
