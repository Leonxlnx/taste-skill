Moments

15 creative moments for tech, gaming and software pages, one or two a page, never on a place page (references/moments.md). Each link is a full demo page; the moment is marked data-moment in its source.

  • Counter rolls text · statement · enter · tech, gaming, software

    The digits of two to four proof figures roll once like an odometer when the row comes on screen, the right-most digit turning most; the strips rest on the real figures before and after, so the row never reads as zeros.

    Phone and reduced motion: The figures stand.

  • Cursor spotlight hover · hero · pointer · tech, gaming, software

    A soft light on the hero's ground follows the pointer with a short lag and shows a fine dot grid around it; it drifts back to its resting spot when the pointer leaves.

    Phone and reduced motion: The light rests at its spot; there is no pointer.

  • Giant type, picture passing scroll · statement · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    Two to four words set at 12vw with a photograph showing through the letters under a 35% ink tint; the photograph passes upward through the type as the section crosses the screen.

    Phone and reduced motion: The photograph stands still in the letters, the type smaller (19vw).

  • Iris opens transition · section · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    A section that sits on a new ground keeps that ground edge to edge; inside it an inset panel in the old ground (even gutter, card corners, never touching the section's edges) opens a disc in the middle of what is visible, widening with a hairline accent ring at its edge. Each heading, paragraph, button and picture stays hidden until the disc holds all of it, then fades in whole, so no word is ever cut.

    Phone and reduced motion: The section stands on its own ground, no panel, no ring.

  • Letters assemble scroll · statement · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    A statement of two to five words, set very large: its letters start scattered, shifted and turned, and fly into the line one after the other as the statement rises through the screen, whole before it reaches the middle.

    Phone and reduced motion: The statement stands as written, in a smaller size.

  • Line carries the colour transition · section · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    A section that sits on a new ground keeps that ground edge to edge; inside it an inset panel in the old ground (even gutter, card corners, never touching the section's edges) is cleared by a one-pixel accent line that climbs through it from its foot to its top: below the line the section's own ground and words, above it the old ground. Each heading, paragraph, button and picture stays hidden until the line has passed above all of it, then fades in whole, so no word is ever cut.

    Phone and reduced motion: The section stands on its own ground, no panel, no line.

  • Lines rise text · section, statement · enter · tech, gaming, software

    One section's heading rises line by line out of masks the first time it comes on screen, 90 ms between lines; its lead, link and visual fade up behind it. Once.

    Phone and reduced motion: The heading and the lines after it stand as written.

  • Liquid hover liquid · hero, section · hover · WebGL · tech, gaming, software

    The pointer pushes a photograph like the surface of water: the picture bends along the pointer's path with a wet shine and a hair of colour split, and the ripple diffuses away in a second or two, back to the plain picture.

    Phone and reduced motion: The plain photograph, no canvas, no WebGL context.

  • Liquid type liquid · hero, statement · pointer · tech, gaming, software

    A very large headline ripples and stretches like type seen through water wherever the pointer crosses it, and settles back to crisp type within about a second; one SVG filter, only while it moves.

    Phone and reduced motion: The headline stands as written; there is no pointer.

  • Marquee row scroll · section · loop · tech, gaming, software

    One row of client words, trades, cities or formats moves slowly sideways in a loop, fading at both edges; it pauses under the pointer and has a pause button.

    Phone and reduced motion: The words stand in a wrapped row; nothing runs.

  • Picture develops transition · section, hero · enter · tech, gaming, software

    One photograph develops once as it comes on screen: a two-tone halftone print in the page's ground and ink, darkest cells first, that gives way to the photograph settling from a slight zoom.

    Phone and reduced motion: The photograph stands.

  • Pinned chapters scroll · section · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    A feature section holds on screen for two screens of scroll while its three steps open one at a time at the left and one frame at the right changes to each step's picture with a short crossfade.

    Phone and reduced motion: Nothing is pinned: each step stands with its title, two lines and its visual under it.

  • Window parallax scroll · section · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    One wide photograph seen through a frame moves a little slower than the page, so the frame reads as a window onto a larger picture.

    Phone and reduced motion: The photograph stands in its frame.

  • Statement lights up scroll · statement · scroll · tech, gaming, software

    One large statement rests at half ink (readable, about 4 to 1) and turns to ink word by word as it crosses the middle of the screen, a strong word to the accent; it runs back on the way up.

    Phone and reduced motion: The whole statement in ink, the accent word in the accent.

  • Word rotates text · hero · loop · tech, gaming, software

    One word of the headline changes every two seconds, sliding up out of its mask as the next rises in, underlined in the accent; three rounds, then it rests.

    Phone and reduced motion: The written word stands; the other words are not shown.
