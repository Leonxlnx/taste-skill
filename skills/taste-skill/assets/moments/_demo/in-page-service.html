Friday 15 May, from Whitby

How the three days go

  1. Day one, 48 km: the moor

    The van takes the bags. You ride over the moor road with the guide ahead and coffee at the top.

  2. Day two, 62 km: the coast

    Along the water on a flat road, lunch at the harbour and the night in a guesthouse by the pier.

  3. Day three, 30 km: the dunes

    A short ride to the dunes, a swim if the weather holds, and the train back from the last station.

What a ride includes

Two nights in guesthouses, every breakfast and lunch, the van and a mechanic.
