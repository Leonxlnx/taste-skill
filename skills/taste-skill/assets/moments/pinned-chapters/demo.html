How a Northbound ride goes

  1. Day one: the pass

    A van takes the bags up. You ride 48 km over the pass on a closed road, with a guide ahead and coffee at the top.

  2. Day two: the coast

    62 km along the water on a flat road, lunch at the harbour, and the night in a guesthouse by the pier.

  3. Day three: the dunes

    A short 30 km to the dunes, a swim if the weather holds, and the train back from the last station.

What a ride includes. From €640 a person.

Two nights in guesthouses, every breakfast and lunch, the van for your bags, a guide and a mechanic. Bring your bike or hire one of ours.
