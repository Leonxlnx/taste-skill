Calhau, a fire kitchen above the bay

Thirty-two seats at the end of the cliff road above Vale Escuro, open from sundown, with everything cooked over olive wood.

The sun goes down, the fire stays up.

Tables from sundown, Wednesday to Sunday

The grill is lit an hour before the first table. Fish comes up from the harbour at five; what is not cooked over the coals that night goes back to the boats as bait.

  • Thirty-two seatsOn the terrace until the end of October, then around the hearth inside.
  • From sundown until the last tableThe kitchen takes its last order at ten.
