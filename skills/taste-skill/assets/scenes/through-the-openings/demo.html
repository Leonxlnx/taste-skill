Twelve rooms, one garden

Casa Brolo is a guesthouse in a former convent at the edge of the old town. The street stays outside the wall; the rooms look in, over the arcade and the lawn.

The garden inside the walls

Days under the arcade

The arcade runs on all four sides, so there is always a dry seat and always a patch of sun. Breakfast is laid under the arches; after dinner the lawn is yours.

  • Breakfast at eightBread from the bakery across the square, eggs from the convent's old orchard.
  • A quiet gateThe door to the street closes at ten. Guests have a key.
  • Friday musicA string quartet on the lawn, June to September.

Stay a few nights

Every room is on the upper floor, with a window over the garden and thick walls that keep the summer out. From 140 euros a night, breakfast included.
