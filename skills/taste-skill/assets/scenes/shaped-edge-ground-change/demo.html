Nine days on the ice

On foot across the Varn glaciers from Ossen to Valtar, with a staffed hut every night. Open 1 July to 14 September.

See the stages

Above the tree line

Seven cols and two glaciers between Ossen and Valtar, all of it on foot.

Where the sky begins

Long days on the snow, warm rooms at night.

Nine huts

One hut a night

Each hut sleeps thirty to sixty and serves dinner, breakfast and a packed lunch. On the two glacier days a guide walks with groups of eight; the rest of the path is marked.

  • Ossen to Kerr Hut11 km, 1,150 m of climbing
  • Kerr Hut to Lammer9 km, 640 m of climbing
  • Lammer to Ebbet Col13 km, 980 m of climbing
  • Ebbet Col to Sander Hut8 km on the glacier, guided
  • Sander Hut to Moll12 km, 720 m of climbing
  • Moll to Hallin Hut10 km, 860 m of climbing
  • Hallin to Tarve Hut7 km on the glacier, guided
  • Tarve Hut to Orn14 km, 520 m of climbing
  • Orn down to Valtar16 km, mostly downhill
