Solvra One, on the quay

A tokamak four metres across, wound from high-temperature superconducting tape and fuelled with deuterium taken from seawater pumped in at the quay wall. The magnets arrive this winter. First plasma is planned for spring 2031, and first power to the Aberdeen grid for 2034.

Size
Four metres across, eleven tall
Fuel
Deuterium from the North Sea, tritium bred on site
Output
Fifty megawatts at full power
First plasma
Spring 2031
