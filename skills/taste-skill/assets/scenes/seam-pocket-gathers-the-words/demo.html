Cut flowers from one small field

Hollin Row is two acres outside Pellbridge, grown without sprays by Ada and Tom Wren. We cut on Friday mornings from June to the first frost and sell the flowers by the bucket.

Order a bucket

What the field grows

Sown in waves from March, so each flower has its few weeks at its best. Everything is cut before eight and stands in the cooler by nine.

  • Zinnias
  • Dahlias
  • Cosmos
  • Sweet peas
  • Rudbeckia
  • Snapdragons
  • Celosia
  • Strawflower
  • Scabious
  • Ammi

Order a Friday bucket

A bucket holds whatever is best in the field that week, cut that morning and bunched by hand. Order by Wednesday evening and collect it from the farm gate on Friday afternoon.
