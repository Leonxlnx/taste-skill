Swim after dark. Let the sea come in.

Lamps on from nine until midnight

  • Lit from below

    Twelve lamps under the waterline, so you can see your hands on every stroke.

  • Fed by the tide

    The sea refills the pool twice a day. No chlorine, no heating, just the bay.

  • Guards on deck

    Two lifeguards on every night shift, and one in the water when the swell rises.

  • Sauna by the steps

    A wood-fired sauna ten paces from the ladder, kept hot until the last swimmer leaves.

Swim tonight

Sessions start every half hour from nine until half past eleven. Bring a towel; hats and lamps are lent at the gate.
