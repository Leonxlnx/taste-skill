Fellhouse builds timber cabins whole in a barn at Aldmere, then drives each one to its plot overnight, ready for the keys by breakfast.

How a delivery night goes

Your cabin, driven home

Every cabin leaves our Aldmere workshop finished, wired and glazed, and travels 410 km to your plot as one wide load, by night and under escort.

Distance driven 410 km

  1. Aldmere workshop

    Twelve weeks of joinery end on a Tuesday evening. The crane sets the finished cabin on the low-loader, and we strap it at eight points and photograph every corner.

    Start, 0 km

  2. Corran harbour

    Wide loads may only move between ten at night and six in the morning. We wait out the evening at the harbour, swap drivers and check every strap again.

    128 km

  3. Hesketh pass

    The steepest climb, one in seven at the top. The road is closed behind us for twenty minutes so the cabin can take the bends on the full width.

    262 km

  4. Your plot at Brannock

    We arrive before first light. By breakfast the crane has set the cabin on its twenty-five ground screws, levelled to two millimetres, and the keys are on your table.

    410 km
