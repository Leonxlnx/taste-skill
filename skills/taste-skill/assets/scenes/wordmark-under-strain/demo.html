Run up the stairs of Porto

980 granite steps from the river to the Batalha, one Sunday in May.

Seven stairways, one finish line

The course climbs the Guindais steps, the Codeçal stairs and five lanes the city keeps for walkers the rest of the year. The fastest runners finish in eleven minutes; most take half an hour, and everyone walks a stretch.

Entries for 18 May 2027 are open: two thousand places, and a start wave every ten minutes from seven.
