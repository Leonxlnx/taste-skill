Skerry Air Freight

Twice a day we carry fish out and post, parts and medicine in, between forty North Atlantic islands and the mainland.

We fly when the ferries can't.

Islands that count on us

  • “The harbour was shut for nine days in February. The insulin still came in on Tuesday, and so did the post.”

    Ingrid Hallsdóttir, pharmacist on Ravnsey

  • “Our crab leaves the pier at five and is on a plate in Lisbon by dinner. Before Skerry, a third of it went back into the sea.”

    Tór Mikkelsen, fish exporter on Fjarholm

One freighter, one hangar on Fjarholm, and pilots who know every island runway by its wind.

Send it on the 06:10

Pallets close at eight the evening before. Tell us what it is, what it weighs and which island it is going to, and we will hold a place on the 06:10.
