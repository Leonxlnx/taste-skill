Skerrow Sea Swimmers

Since 1987 a few hundred people have met on the north pier at seven each morning and gone into the North Sea, whatever it is doing.

Book a first swim

In the sea at seven, every day of the year.

1987 the first winter

Four swimmers, one flask and no wetsuits.

  • 365Mornings a year
  • 412Members this winter
  • 6°Coldest sea on record
  • 39Winters on the pier

Seven o'clock, at the north pier steps.

Your first swim is on a Saturday, with a member beside you the whole way. Bring a towel, a warm hat and something hot in a flask. The club brings the kettle and counts heads.

When
Every morning at seven, all year
Where
The steps at the end of the north pier
Membership
Twenty pounds a year, for the lifebuoy and the kettle
