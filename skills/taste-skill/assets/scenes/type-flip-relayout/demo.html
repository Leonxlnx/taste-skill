Work

  • Menus and maps
  • Wedding cards

Each job starts at the type cases, four hundred drawers of wood and lead. We set it by hand and print it deep enough to feel.

Come and see the presses run

Tramstraat 14, 9000 Ghent. Bring the paper you want to print on and we will tell you what it can take and what a run of it costs.

Open Thursday to Saturday, ten to five. Printing days are Monday to Wednesday, when the shop is closed and the presses are loud.
