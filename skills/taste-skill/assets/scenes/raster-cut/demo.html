Awake by the sea

Awake by the sea

The blinds rise at 06:40, when the line meets the shore, and breakfast is served until the last stop at Halvik.

Three nights a week, all year

Kestrel leaves the city on Tuesdays, Fridays and Sundays. Cabins sleep one, two or four, with fresh linen, a washbasin and a window on the sea side for the last two hours of the line.

Breakfast in the dining car

Coffee from 06:30, rye bread from the bakery at the halfway stop, and a table by the window for as long as the coast lasts.

Kestrel Night Line runs between the city and Halvik. Tickets open ninety days before departure, and dogs travel free in cabins for one.
