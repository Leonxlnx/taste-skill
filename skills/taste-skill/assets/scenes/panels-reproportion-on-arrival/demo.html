Open Saturdays

On the first Saturday of every month the workshops are open from ten until four. Watch a class at the wheel or the press, talk to the teachers, and try a bench for an hour.

The school is in the old rope works on the river, ten minutes on foot from the station, with a café in the yard and room for bikes inside the gate.

  • A whole year, September to July, five days a week
  • Tuition with materials and firing: £8,600
  • Bursaries cover half the fee for half the places
  • No portfolio needed, only the wish to learn by doing
