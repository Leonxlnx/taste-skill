Slow films, cold water

Two people, one camera and a van. Our new film watched a single waterfall every morning for a whole year.

A year spent behind one waterfall

Thaw

Filmed from the cave behind the falls, from one thaw to the next, then cut into ninety-four minutes.

Thaw

Thaw opens on 14 November

Ninety-four minutes, no narration, one waterfall and the light that moves through it. The first screenings are at the Harbour Picture House in Wellmouth, and both of us will stay afterwards to talk about how it was made.
