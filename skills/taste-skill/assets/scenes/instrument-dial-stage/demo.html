Fjord to summit in two days

Varde guides ropes of four up the Skarvan ridge every summer weekend, with one night in the hut at the edge of the glacier.

One climb, four heights

    Set off at six

    640 m Car park by the fjord

  2. Forest

    Lunch under the pines

    1,180 m Where the trees thin out

  3. Glacier

    Roped up at dawn

    2,350 m Hut at the edge of the ice

  4. Summit

    On top by noon

    3,060 m Cairn on the north top

Ropes leave every Saturday at six

From June to September, four climbers and one guide to a rope. Boots, crampons, the harness and the hut night are ours; bring a warm layer and your own lunch for the first day.
