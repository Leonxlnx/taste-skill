Wooden boats, one at a time

Three of us build open clinker boats on the old west-coast lines: larch planks on oak frames, copper rivets, tar and linseed oil. These are this year's three.

Twelve weeks on the slip

Every boat is built to order on the slip beside the boathouse. You come three times: when the keel is laid, when the last plank goes on, and for the launch.
