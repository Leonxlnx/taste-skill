Wren Street Joinery

Tables, benches and desks in oak and ash, made one at a time in a workshop behind Wren Street. About five weeks from the timber yard to your room.

Every table starts out rough

  1. Week one

    Rough-sawn oak

    We pick the boards at the yard ourselves. Then they settle in the shop for a week before the first cut.

  2. Weeks two and three

    Cut and fitted

    Mortise and tenon, cut by hand and dry-fitted twice. Nothing is glued until every joint closes under the push of a palm.

  3. Week four

    Five grits of sand

    From 80 grit to 320, always with the grain. Each pass takes out the scratches the pass before it left behind.

  4. Week five

    Oiled by hand

    Three coats of hard-wax oil, rubbed in and buffed off. The top ends up feeling like a river stone under your arm.

Come and see the boards first

The workshop is open on Saturday mornings. Bring the size of your room and how many sit at the table, and we will show you the boards we would cut it from.
