A dining room on the harbour wall

Kittiwake cooks what the boats and the farms behind Saltcote bring in each morning. One menu, written at noon, served from seven.

This week

Mussels steamed in cider with sorrel. Hake on leeks and brown butter. Crab on rye toast, quince with burnt cream.

Seven courses at one long table on Friday, or three from the board any night.

At the end of the harbour wall

Where

The old net loft, Harbour Wall, Saltcote. The door faces the slipway; the room is up one flight of stairs.

When

Wednesday to Sunday from seven. The long table on Fridays, lunch on Saturdays from twelve.

Tables

For two to eight, held up to six weeks ahead. Four seats at the bar are kept for walking in.

The menu

Written each day at noon and on the board by five. Tell us what you cannot eat when you book.
