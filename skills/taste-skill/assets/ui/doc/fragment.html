Returns policy, draft 3
4. Returns and refunds
You can return any item within 30 days of delivery. It must be unused and in its original box.
We refund the price of the item to the card you paid with. Return shipping is free for orders inside the EU.
Items bought on sale can be exchanged, but not refunded. Gift cards cannot be returned.
Refunds reach your account within 5 working days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
