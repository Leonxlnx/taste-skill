# Block Families

These are structural families, not finished inventory. A new family must materially change at least two of: content hierarchy, grid topology, copy and media relationship, navigation or disclosure model, interaction model, and narrative progression.

Mirrors, themes, colors, fonts, radii, item counts, media formats, and simple reveal animations are variants.

## Ownership rules

- Hero introduces; a call to action closes or advances after new context.
- Introduction explains the subject; About explains identity and history.
- Services state the offer; How It Works explains its sequence or mechanism.
- Selected Work indexes projects; Gallery presents media; Case Study explains one project.
- Clients proves relationships; Trust proves credibility; Results presents measured outcomes.
- Contact routes a conversation; Signup completes registration, booking, application, or donation.
- Footer closes and orients. It is not another hero.
