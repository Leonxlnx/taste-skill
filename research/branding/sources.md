# Branding sources

## Standards and accessibility

- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) — text and non-text contrast, reflow, resize, use of color, animation, and input accessibility.
- [WCAG: contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) — 4.5:1 normal text and 3:1 large text.
- [WCAG: non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) — 3:1 for meaningful controls, states, and graphics.
- [WCAG: text spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/text-spacing.html) — content must survive user spacing overrides.
- [CSS Color 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/) — OKLCH/Oklab, wide gamut, and gamut mapping.
- [CSS Fonts 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-fonts-4/) — variable axes, optical sizing, font loading, metrics, and feature controls.
- [WOFF2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WOFF2/) — modern webfont format.
- [CSS Color Adjustment](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-adjust-1/) — forced colors and color-scheme behavior.
- [Design Tokens glossary](https://www.designtokens.org/glossary/) — primitive, semantic, and component-token terminology.

## Official design systems and brand guidance

- [USWDS color](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/color/overview/) and [theme tokens](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-tokens/color/theme-tokens/) — role-based color and the limited meaning of 60/30/10.
- [Adobe Spectrum color system](https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/color-system/), [using color](https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/using-color/), and [states](https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/states/) — intentional chroma, paired foregrounds, interaction states, transparency, and themes.
- [Carbon color](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/color/overview/), [color tokens](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/color/tokens/), and [type](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/typography/overview/) — semantic color and productive versus expressive typography.
- [Atlassian design tokens](https://atlassian.design/foundations/tokens/design-tokens/), [color](https://atlassian.design/foundations/color-new/), [type](https://atlassian.design/foundations/typography/product-typefaces-and-scale), and [icons](https://atlassian.design/foundations/iconography) — token architecture and coherent visual roles.
- [IBM 2x Grid](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/2x-grid/overview/), [photography](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/photography/overview/), [illustration](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/illustration/tips-and-techniques/), [pictograms](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/pictograms/usage/), and [data visualization](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/data-visualization/overview/) — composition, art direction, visual grammar, and honest information graphics.
- [GitLab brand voice](https://design.gitlab.com/brand-messaging/brand-voice/), [photography](https://design.gitlab.com/brand-design/photography/), [illustration](https://design.gitlab.com/product-foundations/illustration), and [iconography](https://design.gitlab.com/product-foundations/iconography/) — verbal behavior and asset-system consistency.
- [Apple branding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding), [design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles), and [dark mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode) — restrained expression, asset integrity, and theme adaptation.
- [GOV.UK typography](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/typography/), [type scale](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/type-scale/), and [images](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/images/) — functional hierarchy, readability, images, and icons.
- [Spotify brand redesign](https://spotify.design/article/making-the-brand-redesigning-spotify-design) and [Encore](https://spotify.design/article/reimagining-design-systems-at-spotify) — identity translation and scalable multi-surface systems.
- [Google DESIGN.md](https://github.com/google-labs-code/design.md) — useful AI-facing split between normative tokens, explanatory intent, and lintable checks; Apache-2.0.
- [Primer Brand](https://github.com/primer/brand) — open marketing-system architecture; MIT. Use the architecture, not GitHub's trade dress.
- [Radix Colors](https://github.com/radix-ui/colors) — role-oriented light, dark, and alpha scales; MIT.
- [Spectrum Design Data](https://github.com/adobe/spectrum-design-data) — structured primitive, semantic, and component tokens; Apache-2.0.

## Typography and font engineering

- [Google: choosing web fonts](https://design.google/library/choosing-web-fonts-beginners-guide) — family coverage, text/display roles, contrast, and pairing.
- [Google Fonts variable-font guide](https://googlefonts.github.io/gf-guide/variable.html) — axes and compatibility.
- [web.dev font best practices](https://web.dev/articles/font-best-practices) — WOFF2, subsetting, preload restraint, and delivery.
- [OpenType variable-font overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/otvaroverview) and [axis registry](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/typography/opentype/spec/dvaraxisreg) — registered axes and ranges.
- [SIL Open Font License](https://openfontlicense.org/) and [FAQ](https://openfontlicense.org/ofl-faq/) — commercial use, redistribution, modification, notices, and Reserved Font Names.
- [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts), [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/), [League of Moveable Type](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/), [Collletttivo](https://www.collletttivo.it/licensing), and [Velvetyne](https://velvetyne.fr/) — reputable open-font sources. Verify each exact binary and license.
- [Adobe Fonts licensing](https://helpx.adobe.com/fonts/using/font-licensing.html) and [webfont licensing](https://helpx.adobe.com/fonts/using/webfont-licensing.html) — service-hosted use is not permission to redistribute or self-host font files.
- [Fontshare licenses](https://fontshare.com/licenses) — open and proprietary families have materially different reuse rights.

## Reuse and legal notes

- Do not copy visual designs from Awwwards, studio sites, or brand guidelines unless their exact assets/code are separately licensed for that use.
- Open-source UI code does not grant rights to logos, mascots, trademarks, photography, copy, or trade dress.
- Preserve license and copyright files for redistributed fonts and design-system code.
- For paid/service fonts, require the user or client to hold the correct license; otherwise select an open alternative.
- Never bundle trial, demo, personal-use, OS-extracted, or unattributed font files.

- [FiraGO upstream repository and license](https://github.com/bBoxType/FiraGO) — exact source for the open multiscript family; verify the selected release and bundled license before redistribution.
